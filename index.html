PDF & Doc Tools Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

PDF & Doc Tools is a document reader and PDF editor for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: PDF & Doc Tools does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal data.

No internet access

PDF & Doc Tools does not request the Android internet permission. It cannot send your documents or any other information off your device.

Your documents

Documents you open, edit or create are read and written only on your device. Files you create (for example merged, signed or converted PDFs) are saved to your device's Documents/PDF & Doc Tools or Pictures/PDF & Doc Tools folders. PDF & Doc Tools never uploads them.

Storage permission

If you allow it, PDF & Doc Tools uses the "All files access" permission only to find and list the documents on your device so you can open them in one place. You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in Android Settings; PDF & Doc Tools then works through the system file picker.

Information kept on your device

PDF & Doc Tools keeps a list of recently opened and starred documents, your reading position, bookmarks, saved signatures and your settings. This information stays on your device, may be included in your device's own backup if you enable Android backup, and can be cleared in the app's Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Document scanning

The "Scan to PDF" feature uses the Google Play services document scanner, which runs on your device. PDF & Doc Tools receives only the scanned pages you choose to save.

Ads, analytics and tracking

PDF & Doc Tools contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries.

Children

PDF & Doc Tools does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
